Maintenance vs. total operating expenses, per share

Maintenance per share Total operating expenses per share 2026–27 expense estimate (shaded: higher-growth case)

For 2027 the set maintenance rate is $23.77 a share. Operating expenses are estimated at $21.12 (or $22.12 with faster 6% growth), which leaves about $1.65–$2.65 a share ($140k–$225k) above operating costs. Other income (parking, laundry, storage, interest) added about $3.00 a share in 2025. Part of the gap goes to mortgage principal (≈ $0.96 a share in 2027) and to capital projects, and neither counts as an operating expense.

How 2026–2027 were estimated, and the data behind the chart
  • Maintenance: 2026 and 2027 use the board-set rates ($20.43; $20.43 × 1.1635 = $23.77). For 1990–2025 the chart uses audited maintenance collected ÷ 85,027. Those years include partial-year effects (2005, 2008, 2013).
  • Expenses excluding mortgage interest: 2025 audited ($1,581,474) grown at the 2019–2025 annual rate of 3.43%. The shaded high case uses 6% a year.
  • Mortgage interest: projected from the 5.17% amortization schedule ($107.9k in 2026, $103.9k in 2027).
  • Operating expenses are the audited line items: RE taxes, utilities, payroll, mortgage interest, repairs, insurance, security, management, water/sewer, legal/audit and corporate tax/misc. Capital expenditures and mortgage principal are not included.
